# Commercial use

How to ask for permission to use lfphys commercially. This file describes the procedure; it is not a licence or an
agreement, and it does not change the full licence texts.

## What needs a separate agreement

* **Code** (`LICENSE.md`, lfphys Research Licence 1.0, text of 14 November 2026): any Commercial Use as defined in its
  section 1, item 4, by anyone and whatever the user's status — a company, a university or an individual. Its items, in
  short (the licence's words decide): use by or for a business (4.1); research or computation that a business pays for,
  requests or directs, and sponsored research in which a business obtains rights in, an option on, exclusive access to,
  or access before publication to the results (4.2); including the software in a product or a service offered for a fee
  or in support of a commercial activity, also over a network (4.3); distribution of the software, or access to it, for
  a fee (4.4); and selling data sets that consist substantially of its output (§6.2). Evaluation by a business before
  asking for an agreement is also commercial use.
* **Documentation**, including the materials of the public site (`LICENSE-DOCS.txt`, CC BY-NC-SA 4.0): use primarily
  intended for or directed towards commercial advantage or monetary compensation, as that licence defines it by the
  purpose of the use, not by who uses it. The additional permissions of `PUBLICATIONS.md` (scholarly publications,
  including through commercial publishers) need no agreement.

Noncommercial research, study, teaching and outreach need no agreement (`LICENSE.md` §1, items 3 and 5), and neither
does hosting the software free of charge for others' noncommercial use (with the notices of `LICENSE.md` §4.3).

## How to ask

Write to idrisov.sci@gmail.com: who You are, what You intend to do with the software, at what scale and for how long.
A commercial licence may be granted by a separate written agreement signed by the Licensor. A request alone grants no
permission, and the Licensor is not obliged to grant one.

The Licensor is Ildar Idrisov, an individual; there is no company behind the project at present. The agreement sets
its own terms: the scope, the fees, the warranties and their exclusions, the limit of liability, the verification of
results, support, the parties, the governing law and the resolution of disputes. The disclaimer of the noncommercial
licence does not by itself bind a commercial licensee.

A commercial agreement covers only rights the Licensor holds. Third-party components (`NOTICE`) and the dependencies
the software installs keep their own licences (`lfphys/sources/dependencies.md`).
