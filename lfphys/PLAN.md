## 1. Working mode

* **Batches of three items.** One batch per pass: implementation with tests, an independent physics review by a
  different agent and its processing (`../AGENTS.md` §3 rules 5, 6), then a report to the owner and a discussion. The next
  batch starts only on the owner's go. The order below is a proposal; the owner may change it at any discussion.
* **Rules that hold for every item.**
  * Layers (`LAYERS.md`): established physics with its stated domain and error goes to the core; simplified forms of
    laws the core has in fuller form go to `theories/approx`; fitted phenomenology to `theories/models`; refuted or
    deliberately changed laws to `theories/alternatives`. Every module carries its `ABOUT` passport.
  * Every function is tested against a closed form or a second, independent method; certified (Arb) references where
    a quantity allows it; tolerances derived, not fitted.
  * Licence-clean code (item 12, `../AGENTS.md` §3 rule 8): own implementations from published descriptions; permissive
    runtime dependencies only (weak copyleft allowed unmodified as a separate library); third-party physics libraries
    only in comparison tests (item 13).
  * Open problems found on the way go to `../STATUS.md`, section V (one place).
  * The scope proposed below for the very large fields (items 2, 3, 4, 8) is a first, verifiable subset; the
    owner decides the scope at the start of that batch.

### Playgrounds (P15; owner, 2026-10-28)

Two modes in the player. **Demonstrations**: the prepared visualizations (v01, v02, …: a set-up, its checks, a story);
they stay as they are. **Playground**: a research sandbox, one per section of physics. A playground starts empty (or
from a saved scene); the researcher adds objects from the section's palette, sets and changes their parameters live,
and watches them move and interact. Nothing is scripted: the scene is advanced in real time by the section's solver
from the engine's laws, every visible consequence is computed by the run (`../AGENTS.md` §3 rules 2–4 hold as for the
demonstrations: accepted physics by default, a changed law is a named and flagged world parameter; consequences
revealed as the run's time reaches them). Each section is split by the mechanics it researches — one solver family
and one set of conservation laws per section, so that what is seen can be checked.
