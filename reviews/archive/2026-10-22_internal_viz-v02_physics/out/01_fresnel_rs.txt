== 4. RS kernel: sign and magnitude ==
  wide aperture (0.4 mm, z=2 mm, Fresnel number 80): U_RS(0,z)/exp(ikz) = -0.9423+0.0413j   (expected ~ +1 up to edge ripple)
  U_RS / (sqrt(k/(2 pi i z)) e^{ikz} A_F) at x = 0, 1, 3 mm: [-1.    -0.j     -1.    +0.0002j -0.9999+0.0126j]
  analytic: int_R H1^(1)(k sqrt(x^2+z^2)) z/sqrt(x^2+z^2) dx = -2i e^{ikz}/k  ->  RS-I kernel = +(i k z/2) H1/R;
  rs.py uses -(i k z/2) H1/R (docstring and code), i.e. an overall factor -1 (no effect on |U|^2).
