### C1 (error) — far-future a(t) silently wrong; the ODE route crashes — **fixed** [error in earlier]

* **Reproduced** (`r09`): w = −0.99 gave a = 2.05e116 for 1e70; `scale_factor_ode` raised IndexError beyond
  t − t0 = 3186 Gyr.
* **Cause and fix** (`flrw.py`). S is no longer formed where it over- or underflows: `_terms(a, q)` divides every term
  by a^q with q the power of the term largest at a (`_q`, compared through logarithms; 4 where Λ dominates), and the
  integrands of t, η, r_s (`_integrand`, `_integrand_s`), dt/d ln a in the inversion and the acceleration equation
  carry the matching powers of a. A dark-energy term is formed as the direct product where both factors are normal
  numbers and through logarithms otherwise; the w0-wa model gained `log_density_ratio` (`dark_energy.py`) for that.
  The inversion (`_invert`) now takes a bisection step when the derivative is not finite and positive (the old code
  took a zero step and called it convergence), accepts only when the step or the bracket is below the tolerance
  and the residual |t(a) − t| is within that step's reach plus 1e-9 (|t| + 1/E) (a guard, far above the integrals'
  error), and raises FloatingPointError otherwise; a time whose a lies outside [1e-300, 1e300] gives inf (or 0) with
  a RuntimeWarning; the grid reaches 1e300. `scale_factor_ode` checks the solver's status: after a failure the times
  give nan with a RuntimeWarning. `_adaptive` raises only for an undefined integrand (E² ≤ 0) and accepts an
  overflowing one (inf: the integral exceeds binary64).
* **Verification.** `test_far_future_scale_factor_closed_forms`: (i) flat matter + Λ, ln a = (1/3) ln(O_m/O_L) +
  (2/3) ln sinh x to t = 10000 Gyr (a ~ 1e260), within the derived tolerance E(a)(err_t + 4u τ) + 4u max(1, |ln a|) +
  8u |ln a|; the ODE route at a ~ 1e74, 1e124 within 1e-9; 20000 Gyr gives inf with the warning, the ODE nan with
  its warning; (ii) w = −0.99 alone, ln a = ln(q H0 t)/q, a = 1e-200 … 1e267 (and 1e333 → inf); (iii) matter + w =
  −0.99 (the reviewer's case) against 30-digit mpmath t(a) at a = 1e50, 1e70, 1e120. Negative control
  `test_scale_factor_inversion_guard`: a t(a) with a jump of 1e-6/H0 makes the guard raise (the old acceptance would
  have returned an a). `r09` re-run: Planck 2018 at 3200, 5000, 10000 Gyr within 1.4e-14 … 2.9e-14, the ODE route now
  agrees; t(a = 1e80) = 3220.0438 Gyr (the de Sitter continuation, was 3102.3); w = −0.99 at 1e50 … 1e120 within
  4.5e-14 of the reviewer's reference (was 46 orders off). `r08` re-run: 4.7e-14 at 5000 Gyr, unchanged.

### C2 (should-fix) — `roots()` misses narrow forbidden bands — **fixed** [error in earlier]

* **Reproduced** (`r06`, `r02` D): ε = 1e-6, 1e-8 gave `roots() = (None, None)` and the generic error.
* **Fix** (`roots`). Besides the sign scan, S is minimised (bounded Brent) between the neighbours of every local
  minimum of the scanned values whose three-point parabola dips below half its value (noise on a plateau does not),
  and for a polynomial S (Λ, dust, curvature, radiation) also at the exact stationary points (real roots of S' = O_m +
  2 O_k a + 4 O_L a³); the band edge next to a = 1 is polished by brentq. Residual (docstring): a band of a
  non-polynomial S is missed only if S has two extrema within one scan step (Δ ln a ≈ 0.014); proposed for STATUS
  as a stated limitation.
* **Verification.** `test_narrow_forbidden_band_found` (ε = 1e-6, 1e-8; Λ and the general route through a w0-wa
  model with (−1, 0)): the bounce against the cubic's roots by 40-digit mpmath `polyroots` within the conditioning
  (the engine's stated rounding of S over |S'|); `age()` raises "no big bang"; a(t) since today inverts 30-digit
  mpmath t(a) at a = 0.46, 0.5, 0.8; agreement with the ODE route within 1e-11/√ε (the ODE's error grows through the
  near-static phase: 6e-9 at ε = 1e-8, measured). Negative control: ε = −1e-8 (loitering, no band) keeps its big bang.

### C3 (should-fix) — integrable singularity at the big bang: the estimate not a bound, then a false crash — **fixed** [error in earlier]

* **Reproduced** (`r10`): η at w = −0.22 actual/stated 1.06; failures from w = −0.24 (η) and −0.9 (t).
* **Fix.** For a ≤ 4 every segment (not only the first) is integrated in s with ξ = √a = s^k, k = (m + 1)/(1 + α),
  m = ceil(1 + α) − 1 (`_bang_segments`): the leading power 2ξ^α becomes the polynomial 2k s^m, which Gauss–Legendre
  integrates exactly; the corrections are powers s^(m+β), β > 0, for which the recorded difference bounds the error
  (the error after a halving is 1/(2^(1+β) − 1) ≤ 1 of it). S/a^p is formed termwise (no underflow; where a = s^(2k)
  itself underflows, a = 1e-300 is used and its bound added to the rounding). k = 1 for radiation, matter, curvature,
  Λ (unchanged numerics). The `_adaptive` docstring now states the condition under which the recorded difference is
  a bound.
* **Verification.** `test_dark_energy_dominated_big_bang_closed_forms`: η for w = −0.22, −0.26, −0.3, −0.32 and t for
  w = −0.86, −0.9, −0.95, −0.99 at 11 redshifts against the closed forms with `_check_err` (value within 1e-12, the
  stated error not below the actual one, ≤ 1e-12). Negative control of the reason: for 2x^α on one panel the
  difference to its halves is smaller than the halves' actual error at α = −0.66, larger at α = 0.5. `r10` re-run:
  every case passes, actual/stated ≤ 0.17; `r02` A: 27 checks, none failing (w = −0.3, −0.32 η and −0.9, −0.95 t
