The browser check's own method (found while doing this): screenshots of canvases differ by one level in single pixels
between two shots of an unchanged canvas (the page's compositor), and the GPU path of 2-D canvases (SwiftShader)
antialiases the same commands differently from one drawing to the next. The check therefore runs Chromium with
`--disable-accelerated-2d-canvas`, reads the canvases' pixels, and draws the view once more at the same cursor before
reading it. With that, no visualization needs a "known effect" exception any more (results below).
